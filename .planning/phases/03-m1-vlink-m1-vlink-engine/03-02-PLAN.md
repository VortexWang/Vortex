<success_criteria>
The link supports multiple independent streams with exact ID, lifecycle, and window behavior; stream failures do not corrupt the link except for configured cumulative thresholds.
</success_criteria>

<threat_model>
- T-03-04: stream ID collision or reuse confuses authorization; parity, monotonic allocation, and no reuse mitigate.
- T-03-05: unbounded buffering or window races cause memory exhaustion; per-stream and aggregate credit with cancellation mitigate.
- T-03-06: one malformed stream kills unrelated traffic or remains open after identity failure; explicit failure scope mitigates.
</threat_model>
